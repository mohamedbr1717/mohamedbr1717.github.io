Privacy Policy — ALIX Calendar

Effective: 11 October 2026. Personal-use tool; not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

ALIX Calendar accesses the owner's own Google Calendar data (event titles, times, and descriptions) through the Google Calendar API, solely to perform actions the owner explicitly requests — such as listing, creating, or deleting calendar events.

How data is used and stored

Calendar data is processed on the owner's own private virtual server in order to carry out the requested action (for example, replying through a personal Telegram bot). Data is not copied to any other system and is not used for any other purpose.

Data sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party. The only external communication is with Google's own APIs, which is required for the tool to function.

Data retention

Operational logs on the private server may briefly contain event details needed to confirm an action; they are kept only for troubleshooting and are not shared.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the owner at the developer email address listed on the Google Cloud project's OAuth consent screen.
